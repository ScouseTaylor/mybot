Privacy Policy

Discord TikTok Notifications

Last updated: 14 September 2026

This policy explains how Discord TikTok Notifications uses information when connecting an authorized TikTok account to Discord notification features.

Information used

The service may process TikTok account identifiers and public video information made available through TikTok's authorized APIs. It also uses Discord server, channel, and role identifiers needed to deliver configured notifications.

How information is used

Information is used only to provide the requested integration, such as identifying new public TikTok videos and sending notifications to the configured Discord channel.

Sharing

The service does not sell personal information. Information is shared with TikTok and Discord only as necessary for the integration to communicate with those services, subject to their own policies.

Storage and security

Configuration information needed to operate the bot may be stored for as long as the integration is configured. Reasonable measures should be used to protect authentication credentials and access tokens. Credentials are not intended to be publicly disclosed.

Your choices

You can stop using the integration, remove its Discord configuration, or revoke TikTok authorization. Revoking authorization may prevent TikTok notification features from working.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated as the service or platform requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator through the ScouseTaylor GitHub account.
